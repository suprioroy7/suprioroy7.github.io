About Me

I am an M.Tech student in Computational and Experimental Mechanics at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras), with a background in Mechanical Engineering and Computational Mechanics. My research interests include computational solid mechanics, computational materials science, molecular dynamics, and numerical modelling of material behaviour.

My research experience includes investigating deformation and failure mechanisms in elastic double-network materials through computational modelling, exploring Fourier Neural Operators (FNO) for predicting crystal elasticity and plasticity, and studying the mechanical behaviour and microstructural evolution of metallic materials using molecular dynamics simulations.

I have hands-on experience with LAMMPS, OVITO, Python, Abaqus, MATLAB, SigmaPlot, and scientific data analysis. My research contributions include a Q1 journal article and a conference publication. I am interested in developing computational approaches to understand complex material behaviour, deformation mechanisms, and failure processes across different length scales.
