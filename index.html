About Me

I am a postgraduate student pursuing an M.Tech in Computational & Experimental Mechanics at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras), India. I hold a bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering with a specialization in Computational Mechanics. My research interests lie in computational solid mechanics, computational materials science, molecular dynamics simulations, and scientific machine learning. I have research experience in atomistic simulations of metallic materials using LAMMPS and OVITO, along with numerical modelling and scientific computing using Python. I am interested in developing computational approaches to understand material behaviour, deformation mechanisms, and failure processes across different length scales.
